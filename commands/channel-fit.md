Determine channel-product fit for $ARGUMENTS.

1. Identify the **spread mechanism** using the table in
   `data/channel-economics.md`: collaborative, shareable output, communication,
   single-player utility, marketplace, or open-source infrastructure.
2. Rule out the channels that do not fit, **and say why**. This is the valuable
   half; a ranked list of nine channels is a non-answer.
3. Recommend **one or two** channels with the realistic return from
   `data/channel-economics.md` and the effort required.
4. Name the compounding placements being skipped — registries, awesome-lists,
   topics, docs SEO. These are permanent and most people never do them.

If the product is single-player utility, say directly that it will not be viral
and that referral mechanics would be wasted work.
