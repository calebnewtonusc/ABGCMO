---
description: Research competitors honestly and turn it into a decision
---

Run competitive analysis on $ARGUMENTS using `skills/competitive-intelligence`.

1. **Start with alternatives, not competitors.** What would this buyer do if none
   of these existed? Include doing nothing, a spreadsheet, and manual work — ~40%
   of B2B deals are lost to "no decision."
2. **Pull real signals**, not marketing pages. For open source use `gh` to get
   forks, issue response time, contributor count, and last release. Stars are a
   lagging popularity snapshot.
3. **Name where each competitor is genuinely better.** Refusing to do this makes
   everything else you say untrustworthy.
4. **Find the structural constraint** — what can they not do without breaking
   their own model? That is the durable opening.

Output a decision, not a grid:
- The segment where this product is obviously first
- What to stop building because a competitor owns it
- What to say on a comparison page, including where to send people elsewhere

If the conclusion is "add features to match," re-read the skill. The output should
more often be a narrowing decision than a building one.
