description: Design a growth loop, or establish that the product does not have one
---

Design a growth loop for $ARGUMENTS using `skills/growth-loops`.

1. **Identify the spread mechanism** from `data/channel-economics.md`:
   collaborative, shareable output, communication, single-player utility,
   marketplace, or open-source infrastructure.
2. **If it is single-player utility with no shareable artifact, stop and say so.**
   The product does not have a loop, referral mechanics would be wasted work, and
   it grows through search, registries, and word of mouth instead. That is a
   legitimate outcome and saying it is more useful than designing a loop that
   cannot exist.
3. **Otherwise answer the one question:** what does a new user do that produces
   the next user?

If designing a shareable artifact, use Wordle as the reference: encodes the result
without spoiling it, needs no account, travels as plain text, is socially safe to
post, and contains no ask. Design for what makes **the sharer** look good.

Output:
- The loop, drawn as a cycle
- Realistic K (0.3-0.7 is where healthy products land; sustained K > 1 is rare)
- **Cycle time**, and whether shortening it is easier than raising K
- Whether a nomination mechanic beats a share mechanic here
- What to instrument to know if it works
