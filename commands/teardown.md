description: Research and write a Cold-Start-style growth teardown of a company
---

Write a researched growth teardown of $ARGUMENTS.

Research first, using web search. Prioritize primary and near-primary sources:
founder interviews (Lenny's Newsletter, 20VC, First Round Review, a16z), the
company's own posts, filings, and contemporaneous coverage. Marketing blogs are
last resort and get tagged accordingly.

Structure:
1. **Founding context** — who, and what they had that others did not
2. **The pre-traction years**, including what failed. This is the part most
   teardowns skip and the part that carries the lesson.
3. **The inflection** — what specifically changed, with a date
4. **The growth machinery** — pricing, loops, community, distribution
5. **The playbook** — 5-10 numbered, transferable moves
6. **What would not transfer**, and why

Rules:
- Tag every number per `data/evidence-tiers.md`
- Cite the source inline for each significant claim
- Name the survivorship caveat explicitly
- **Find the structural decision**, not the content tactic. In every case in this
  repo the win was structural. If your teardown concludes "they posted
  consistently," you have not found it yet.

Write it to a markdown file in the repo. Do not publish it as an artifact.
