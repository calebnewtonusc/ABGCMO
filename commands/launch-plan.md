description: Build a concentrated launch-day plan
---

Build a launch plan for $ARGUMENTS using `skills/launch-day`.

First run the pre-launch checklist and **report what is not ready**. Do not plan a
launch date for something with no demo GIF, no license, or a 900-line README.

Then produce:
- A pre-launch fix list, ordered by impact on the measured predictors (HN score,
  existing stars, posting hour, documentation quality)
- **One launch date**, with the specific UTC window per channel
- The channels that fit this specific product, and explicitly which ones do not
  and why
- Draft copy for each: HN title, Reddit framing, X thread hook, LinkedIn post
- What to do in the first hour and the first 24 hours
- Realistic expected outcome with the median-vs-mean caveat

Everything fires on one day. Trending ranks velocity.
