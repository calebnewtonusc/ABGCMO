---
description: Measure product-market fit and decide whether growth work is even allowed yet
---

Assess product-market fit for $ARGUMENTS.

1. **Run the Sean Ellis test.** "How would you feel if you could no longer use
   this?" The threshold is **40% "very disappointed"**, benchmarked across ~100
   startups. Below it, companies almost always struggled to grow.
2. **If they have no survey data**, use retention as the proxy: does the cohort
   curve flatten, and at what level? A curve with no floor means no PMF.
3. **If the score is below 40%, do not treat it as a grade.** Use it as a
   segmentation instrument, the way Superhuman did: segment down to the users who
   love it most, find what is different about them, and build for that segment.
   Superhuman went 22% → 33% by segmenting, then 58% within three quarters.

Output:
- The current read, with the evidence
- **Whether acquisition work is allowed yet.** Below the threshold, say plainly
  that acquisition accelerates churn and name what to do instead.
- The segment to narrow to, if narrowing is the answer
- What would have to be true to re-test in 90 days
