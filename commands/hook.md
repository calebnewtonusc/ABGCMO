description: Turn work into posts people actually read

Generate post options for $ARGUMENTS.

First, find the **finding**. Not the announcement. Look for something specific and
hard-won that is not in the documentation — a gotcha that cost real time, a number
nobody else has, a piece of received wisdom the evidence contradicts. If you
cannot find one, say so; there may not be a post here yet.

Then produce, per `skills/content-engine`:
- 3 hook options across different patterns (contrarian, surprising number, named
  frustration, reference material)
- The platform each one fits, and why
- The full draft for the strongest one, native to its platform
- Where the link goes: **X, first self-reply. LinkedIn, nowhere in the post.**

Apply the portability test to every line. If a sentence could move unchanged to
someone else's post, cut it.

Never write "I built X" as a hook.
