## The finding that matters

**Behavior transfers reliably. Specific citations do not.**

Both answers were structurally correct — they refused the wrong question, named
the real constraint, and reached for the right cases. But the hard numbers that
are supposed to be this repo's differentiator (the FTC penalty, the 90.42%
deletion rate, the ICSE sample) did not surface, even though they sit in
`skills/anti-patterns/SKILL.md` in a table.

Two plausible causes, and they are not exclusive:

1. **Prose-buried numbers do not survive summarization.** A figure inside a
   paragraph competes with the paragraph. The X weights, which sit in a table with
   a bolded ratio column, did surface in other testing.
2. **The skill did not instruct the model to cite.** Nothing in the skill body
   said the citation was mandatory rather than available.

Fix applied: `skills/anti-patterns` now opens with an explicit citation
requirement, and the four load-bearing figures are pulled out of prose into a
table with the source on the same line. Re-test needed.

## What this baseline does not establish

- **No control arm.** These were not compared against the same prompts with the
  repo absent, so "the repo caused this" is inference, not measurement.
- **n=2 scored, single model, single run.** No variance estimate.
- **Self-grading risk.** Scored by the same model family that produced the answers.
- **65 of 67 evals unrun.**

Honest status: the harness works, the doctrine transfers, the citation layer is
weaker than intended, and the measurement is preliminary.
