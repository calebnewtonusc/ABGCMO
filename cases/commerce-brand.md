# Commerce and brand

---

## Glossier

Grew out of **Into The Gloss**, a blog — meaning the audience existed before the
product did. That inverted the usual order and is the whole story.

- User-generated content was **33% of in-feed Instagram posts, 42% on TikTok**.
- Micro-influencers and everyday users as ambassadors with promo codes;
  **8% of sales linked directly to ambassador efforts**.
- **Boy Brow was co-created with the community** and launched to an audience that
  felt ownership of the outcome, which produced organic virality rather than
  purchased reach.

**The honest ending:** once Glossier had converted most of its natural audience
from the blog and Instagram following, **organic growth plateaued**. Community-led
growth has a ceiling set by the size of the community, and the transition to paid
acquisition after that is genuinely hard.

## Gymshark

Ambassadors selected for **relevance and authenticity** — people who had built
organic followings through their own fitness journeys, not people with the biggest
numbers. Supplied gear, invited to exclusive events, encouraged to make whatever
content they wanted.

The product itself was designed to be worn **in gyms and on social platforms**,
so ordinary use generated brand visibility. Campaigns like #Gymshark66 asked
followers to share their own journeys; events like the Gymshark World Tour and
LiftMiami converted online community into physical belonging.

**Transferable:** pick ambassadors for credibility with a specific audience, not
follower count. And if the product is visible during normal use, ordinary
customers are a distribution channel.

---

## Integration marketplaces as a channel

The Shopify App Store has been named a **#1 growth channel** by some SaaS
companies. Salesforce AppExchange plays the same role in enterprise. For a SaaS
in the $3-6M range, integrations with major platforms are a recognized growth
stage rather than a nice-to-have.

**Transferable:** if a platform your customers already use has a store, being in
it is distribution you rent rather than build. It is one of the highest-return,
lowest-creativity moves available.
