## The meta-finding

Read across all of them and the pattern is uncomfortable for anyone hoping for a
content calendar: **almost nothing was won by posting more.** Every case turned
on a structural decision — how the thing was described, who was let in, what the
free tier metered, which registry listed it, or who was personally recruited.

Content was downstream in every single case.
