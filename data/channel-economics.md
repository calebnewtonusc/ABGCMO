# Channel economics

What each channel realistically returns, what it costs, and when it is wrong.

| Channel | Realistic return | Cost | Works when | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hacker News front page | 500-2,000 stars / 5-15k visitors | One post; needs 30-50 upvotes in hour 1 | Technical, self-evident in one sentence, genuinely novel | [M]/[A] |
| Official registry / marketplace | Step change | One submission | An ecosystem exists with a curated index | [R] |
| GitHub topics | Permanent discovery baseline | 5 minutes | Always. Free and near-universally skipped | [A] |
| Awesome-list inclusion | Small, permanent, compounding | One PR each | A relevant list exists | [A] |
| Reddit, niche subs | Highly variable | Reputation, 10:1 rule | You are a real participant already | [A] |
| Product Hunt, top 3 | 5-15k visitors, ~1-2% B2B conv. | A launch day | Consumer-ish, visual, demoable | [A] |
| Product Hunt, outside top 10 | <500 visitors | A launch day | Rarely worth it alone | [A] |
| Documentation SEO | 40-60% of a devtool's organic traffic | Months | You have a real product with real usage | [A] |
| Programmatic SEO | Zapier 2.6M/mo; Wise 60M/mo; Nomad List 50k/mo | Months + a dataset | You own a structured dataset that maps to how people search | [R] |
| Newsletter (owned) | Every subscriber, every send | Ongoing writing | You will publish for 12+ months | [R] |
| LinkedIn newsletter | Notifies all subscribers, no link penalty | One-time setup | You have an existing connection base to auto-invite | [A] |
| X replies | 100-200 profile visits/day reported | 10-15 replies/day | You have something specific to add | [A] |
| Cold email | 3.43% avg reply, 8-12% elite | Domain setup + 3wk warmup | You can name a specific buying signal | [R] |
| Paid ads | Amplifies existing conversion | 300-500 EUR to get signal | Organic already converts | [A] |
| Hackathon sponsorship | Often ~80% recruiting value, 20% brand | Real money | You are hiring, not just marketing | [A] |
| Conferences | One hallway conversation > 50 cold emails | Travel + time | Your buyer physically attends | [A] |
| Podcast guesting | 54% of listeners find shows via other shows | Per appearance | Audience overlap is real, not aspirational | [A] |
| Integration marketplaces | Named a #1 channel for some SaaS | Build + listing | Your product plugs into a platform with a store | [A] |

## The concentration rule

GitHub trending ranks on velocity. Product Hunt is a single day. HN has a ~24h
half-life. **All of these reward concentration and punish spreading.** If you
have five channels, fire them on the same day, not across five weeks.

The exception is anything compounding — SEO, registries, awesome-lists,
newsletters. Those are permanent placements; timing does not matter, only doing
them at all.

## Channel-product fit

Before recommending a channel, check the product's spread mechanism:

| Spread mechanism | Example | Channels that fit |
|---|---|---|
| Collaborative (requires inviting) | Figma, Slack, Notion | In-product invites, team seats. Highest K. |
| Shareable output | Loom, Canva, Wordle, Spotify Wrapped | Social, the artifact carries the brand |
| Communication (both sides need it) | Zoom, Calendly | The link IS the ad |
| Single-player utility | CLI tool, solo analytics | Search, registries, community word of mouth. **Not viral. Stop trying.** |
| Marketplace | Uber, Airbnb, Mercor | Geographic/vertical concentration, hard side first |
| Open source infrastructure | Terraform, Next.js | Docs, conference talks, ecosystem, then commercial layer |
