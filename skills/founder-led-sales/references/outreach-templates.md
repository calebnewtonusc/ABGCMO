# Outreach templates

Templates are a starting structure, not a script. The measured difference between
a 3.43% reply rate and a 15-25% one is **referencing a specific buying signal**,
and no template supplies that for you.

## Rules that outrank the copy

- 50-125 words. Longer formats reply ~50% worse.
- One benefit per message. Multiple claims create skepticism.
- 42% of replies come from follow-ups. 80% of sales need 5+ touches.
- 50-100 sends per mailbox per day, maximum.
- Deliverability sets the ceiling. If sends are high and replies are zero, it is
  infrastructure, not copy.

## 1. Signal-triggered (the one that actually works)

> Subject: your [specific thing they just did]
>
> Hi [name] — saw [specific signal: funding round, a role you posted, a feature
> you shipped, a talk you gave]. [One sentence on what that usually creates.]
>
> [One sentence: what you do, framed as removing that specific problem.]
>
> Worth 15 minutes? Happy to just send the [thing] if that is easier.
>
> [You]

## 2. Warm-intro request (30-50% conversion vs. 2-5% cold)

> [Name] — you know [target] at [company]. I am building [one line].
>
> Would an intro be reasonable? Forwardable blurb below so it costs you one line.
>
> ---
> [2-sentence blurb they can forward verbatim]

## 3. Follow-ups (where 42% of replies live)

- **+3 days:** one new piece of information, not "just bumping this."
- **+7 days:** a different angle on the same problem.
- **+14 days:** a relevant artifact — a teardown, a benchmark, a number.
- **+30 days:** the honest close. "Should I stop following up on this?" This one
  gets replies precisely because it is easy to answer.

## 4. The Collison installation

When someone says yes, remove every step between interest and value.

> Great — do you have 10 minutes right now? I will set it up on your machine
> while we talk so you do not have to touch anything.

Interest decays faster than any other resource you have.

## Infrastructure checklist

- [ ] Secondary sending domains (never the primary)
- [ ] SPF, DKIM, DMARC configured and verified
- [ ] ~3 weeks warmup before scaling
- [ ] Bounce rate under 3% (best in class under 1.5%)
- [ ] Spam complaints under 0.1%
- [ ] List verified before sending
