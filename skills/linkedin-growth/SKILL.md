# LinkedIn growth

Ranking shifted from viral reach toward what LinkedIn calls depth and authority.
**Dwell time governs everything**, and the format hierarchy follows from it.

Carousels win because swiping holds attention 15-20 seconds, which is exactly what
the algorithm measures. Spec: portrait 4:5 (1080x1350), 7-10 slides.

## The article finding that reframes long-form strategy

**Articles underperform ordinary posts on reach by about 31%**, because an article
requires a click and a post does not. Posts get roughly 5x the reach.

That is not an argument against writing them. It means **the article is not the
distribution — it is the asset.** Articles:
- drive **3x more profile visits per impression**
- live permanently on the profile instead of dying in 24-48 hours
- get indexed by Google
- are far more likely than short posts to be **cited by AI systems**, because they
  have heading structure, named frameworks, and specific factual claims

**So: write the article as the durable asset, and use a separate feed post as the
delivery vehicle.** Publishing the article and hoping the feed carries it is the
mistake. Expect double-digit-to-low-hundreds reactions from a well-networked
account, not virality.

## Newsletters bypass the feed entirely

The strongest structural advantage on the platform and badly underused.

- A feed post reaches 8-12% of followers after filtering. A newsletter edition
  **notifies every subscriber**.
- Publishing the first edition **auto-invites every existing connection and
  follower** to subscribe. This is a one-time gift the platform gives once.
- New followers get auto-prompted, so the list compounds without further work.
- **No external-link penalty**, because reach is notification-driven.

**Start the newsletter before you build a posting habit, not after.** The
auto-invite is worth more when your connection base is already there.

## Commenting outperforms posting

Of 500 accounts that grew from under 5k to 25k+ followers in Q1 2026, **76% named
strategic commenting as the primary tactic** [A].

- 10-15 substantive comments a day on accounts 2-10x your size
- Reported yield: 50-100 profile visits/day
- Comments over 15 words materially outperform short ones
- Scoring favors comments containing a **specific question, a data point, or a
  disagreement**
- 50 generic comments a day actively damages account standing

## Timing

Tue-Thu, 10 AM-12 PM local as the baseline. The 2026 shift: **late afternoon now
beats morning**, with Wednesday 4 PM the single strongest slot. Correct timing is
worth 30-50% more impressions.

Be present for the first 60-90 minutes. Reply to every comment inside 15 minutes.

## What LinkedIn is actually for

LinkedIn grows **the person**, not the product. Nobody here will star a repo or
install a CLI. It is where career opportunities, partnerships, and credibility
live. Content that works: narrative, a specific decision and what happened, real
numbers, and things only you could have written. Do not cross-post from X.
