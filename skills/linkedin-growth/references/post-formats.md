# LinkedIn post formats

Dwell time governs ranking: 0-3 s dwell yields 1.2% engagement, 61+ s yields
15.6%. A 13x spread on attention alone. Every format decision follows from that.

## Ranked by measured engagement

| Format | Engagement | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| **Document / PDF carousel** | 6.60% | Default. Highest of any format. |
| Native video | 5.60% | You have something to show |
| Text post | baseline | A story that earns a long read |
| Article | 0.69x reach | As the durable **asset**, never as the distribution |

## Carousel spec

- Portrait **4:5, 1080 x 1350** px
- **7-10 slides** (5-15 acceptable)
- Slide 1 is the hook and does 90% of the work
- One idea per slide, large type, readable at thumbnail size
- Final slide: the concrete next action, not "follow me"

## The narrative text post

```
[Hook — one line, a specific claim or number]

[Context — 2-3 short lines, what the situation was]

[The turn — what you did or found]

[Detail — the specific mechanism, with a number]

[What it means for the reader]

[One question that invites a substantive reply]
```

Line breaks matter — dense paragraphs kill dwell time. Aim for 1-3 lines per block.

## The article-plus-post pattern

Articles reach ~31% fewer people than posts because they require a click. So:

1. Write the **article** as the durable asset — permanent on your profile,
   Google-indexed, citable by AI systems because it has headings and sourced claims.
2. Write a **separate feed post** carrying the single sharpest finding.
3. The post is the distribution. The article is the thing that is still working in
   a year.

Do not publish the article and hope the feed carries it.

## Hard rules

- **No external link in the post body** (~-60% reach)
- **No link in the first comment either** — penalized since early 2026. This is
  the inverse of X. Do not carry the habit across.
- Be present for the first 60-90 minutes; that is the evaluation window
- Reply to every comment within 15 minutes (~+30% engagement)
- Tue-Thu, 10 AM-12 PM local, or Wednesday 4 PM

## Comments are the growth engine

10-15 substantive comments a day on accounts 2-10x your size. Over 15 words.
Containing a specific question, a data point, or a disagreement. Reported yield:
50-100 profile visits a day. Fifty generic comments a day damages standing.
