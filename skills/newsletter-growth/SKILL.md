# Newsletter growth

## Why this outranks every social channel long-term

A newsletter is the only audience you own. Social reach is rented and the terms
change without notice — LinkedIn closed the first-comment link workaround, X
zeroed out link posts for non-Premium accounts. A subscriber list survives all of
that.

If someone is choosing where to invest and can only pick one, this is usually it.

## The referral loop is the cheapest growth mechanic in media

**Morning Brew: 100,000 to 1.7 million subscribers in 18 months.** Roughly 30% of
growth from referrals, as much as **80% in the early days**. 4.5M today.

The mechanics, which The Hustle independently converged on:
- **Tiered, branded physical rewards.** Stickers first, then bigger items.
- **A personal referral link at the bottom of every single issue.**
- **Visible progress** toward the next tier.
- **Cost per acquisition around $0.25** — about $1.25 in stickers and postage for
  five subscribers.
- **Push the channels that convert.** Morning Brew found social and SMS referrals
  dwarfed email referrals and pushed those specifically.

The physical reward matters more than its value. People want the sticker because
it proves membership, not because it is worth anything.

## Platform choice

| Platform | Growth mechanism | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Substack** | App feed, topic categories, Recommendations network | Strongest network in the 0-10k range; partial audience ownership |
| **beehiiv** | Recommendations + paid Boosts (bid per subscriber, 20% fee) | Good discovery, can buy growth |
| **Ghost / Kit / MailerLite** | None built in | **Full ownership**, growth entirely from owned channels |
| **LinkedIn newsletter** | Notification-driven to all subscribers | Lives on LinkedIn; see below |

Below ~10k subscribers, Substack's or beehiiv's network can genuinely move the
needle and the revenue share is worth it. Above that, ownership matters more.

## The LinkedIn newsletter advantage

Structurally the best deal on that platform and badly underused:
- Notifies **every subscriber** vs. a feed post reaching 8-12% of followers.
- Publishing the first edition **auto-invites every existing connection and
  follower**. A one-time gift, given once.
- New followers get auto-prompted, so the list compounds.
- **No external-link penalty**, because reach is notification-driven.
- Platform-wide, LinkedIn newsletters grew 150% YoY to 450M+ subscribers.

**Start it before building a posting habit, not after.** The auto-invite is worth
most when your connection base already exists.

## What to write

The durable formats:
- **A researched teardown** of how something specific worked, with sources. These
  get saved, cited, and forwarded, and they age well.
- **A number nobody else has** — your own data, honestly reported.
- **A decision you made and what happened**, including the parts that went badly.

Cross-post with a **canonical URL** pointing at your own domain. Verbatim
duplication without canonical tags is associated with ~65% organic traffic
decline after six months. Dev.to and Medium both support canonical import;
Medium strips it if you paste manually, so use the import tool.
